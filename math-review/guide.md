**Unit 9.** A quick review of every unit, plus mixed practice. Use it before a test: read
the one-line reminder, and if it feels fuzzy, open that unit's guide.

## One line per unit

| Unit | The one thing to remember |
| --- | --- |
| **1 · Area** | triangle = ½ × base × **perpendicular** height; surface area = add every face |
| **2 · Ratios & %** | divide to get the amount for **one**; percent = per 100 |
| **3 · Fractions & decimals** | dividing fractions = **keep–change–flip**; line up / count decimal places |
| **4 · Expressions & equations** | solve by doing the **inverse to both sides**; then check |
| **5 · Proportional** | y = k·x, k = y ÷ x, graph goes through **(0,0)** |
| **6 · Percent change** | change = % × original; **add** tax/tip, **subtract** a discount |
| **7 · Rational numbers** | **left = less** (−8 < −1); |x| is distance, never negative |
| **8 · Data** | mean = add ÷ count; median = **sort**, take the middle |

## How to study (ADHD-friendly)

1. **One unit at a time.** Read its one-liner above.
2. If you can **say it back** and do one problem, ✓ it and move on.
3. If not, open that unit's guide for 5 minutes, then try again.

## Mixed practice

1. Area of a triangle with base 10, height 6.
2. 25% of 80.
3. ¾ ÷ ½.
4. Solve 5x = 35.
5. A map scale is 1 cm = 10 km. How far is 6 cm?
6. Order from least to greatest: −3, 2, −7, 0.
7. Median of 4, 9, 2, 9, 6.
8. A $50 game is 30% off — final price?
