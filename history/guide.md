## The whole trick, in one line

> **Fact → full answer → check the directions.**

Turn each fact into a complete answer in your own words, then read every direction again
and make sure you did *all* of it before you upload.

## The 4 W's of a research answer

A real answer isn't the title — it's the **title plus four details**. For every event,
make sure you wrote all four:

| Ask | You're answering… | Example detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What** happened? | the event itself | a new bridge opened |
| **When**? | month and year | in March 2024 |
| **Where**? | the place | over the river in Austin, Texas |
| **How did it matter?** | the part people forget | families could reach the hospital in 10 minutes instead of an hour |

> **The one everyone skips is the last one — _how it mattered_.** If you only say *what*
> and *when*, you've restated the title. The points are in the **where** and the **why it
> mattered**.

## Make it 3 complete sentences (in your own words)

Most prompts ask for **at least 3 complete sentences**. One good sentence is **not done** —
it's the start of sentence 1. Grow it:

| Too thin (1 sentence) | Full answer (3 sentences) |
| --- | --- |
| *Mount St. Helens erupted in 1980.* | *In 1980, Mount St. Helens in Washington erupted and blasted ash for miles. The explosion flattened whole forests and killed 57 people. It changed how scientists warn communities before a volcano blows.* |

**In your own words** means: read the source, **look away**, and say it like you'd tell a
friend. Copy-pasting a sentence — even if you change one word — is still copying. Pasting a
long quote from the website is not *your* writing.

- ✅ *Scientists launched the telescope so they could see galaxies that are very far away.*
- 🚫 *"The orbiting observatory was engineered to resolve distant celestial objects."* (lifted from the source)

## Restate the prompt with ACE

When a prompt asks a question, don't start with the bare fact. Use **ACE** — the same
method as every other class:

- **A — Answer** by restating the prompt in a complete sentence.
- **C — Cite / give the details** (what, when, where) with real specifics.
- **E — Explain** how it affected people or the world.

> **Rule:** never open with *"Because…"* or a stray fragment like *"Became the first…"*.
> Start with a **who + did what**: *"Edmund Hillary became the first…"*.

## Check It: the mistakes that sneak in

Whisper-read your answer. If it sounds wrong or doesn't make sense, a mistake is hiding there.

| Wrong | Right | Clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Became the first to reach the top.* (fragment) | *She became the first to reach the top.* | Who is doing it? Add the **who**. |
| *The treaty was signed in 1783 it ended the war.* (run-on) | *The treaty was signed in 1783. It ended the war.* | Two ideas squished — split with a period. |
| *a scoreless 1-0 win* (contradiction) | *a 1-0 win* | Whisper-read: 1-0 isn't scoreless. |
| *he toured with black sabbath* | *he toured with Black Sabbath* | Names of groups and places get **Capitals**. |
| *INSERT WORKS CITED HERE* left in | (replaced with the real list) | A leftover placeholder = not finished. |

**Cite your sources.** Every source you use needs a citation, and they all go on a
**Works Cited** page at the end. AI is not allowed to write it for you — the writing has
to be yours.

## Cheat card (cut out and keep by the homework)

|  | Ask yourself | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Details** | What · When · Where · **How did it matter?** | stopping at the title; no "where" or "why" |
| **Own words** | Did I read it, look away, and say it myself? | pasted sentences; long copied quotes |
| **Check it** | Does every sentence have a who + did what, and make sense? | fragments, run-ons, contradictions, leftover placeholders |
| **Finish** | Did I do **every** direction? (3+ sentences? all parts? sources cited?) | 1 sentence where 3 were asked |

## STOP & CHECK (the finish box — this is the work)

You're not done until **every box is ticked**:

- ☐ Every event has **What · When · Where · How it mattered**.
- ☐ Each description is **at least 3 complete sentences** (I counted).
- ☐ It's in **my own words** — nothing copied, pasted, or AI-written.
- ☐ Every sentence has a **who + did what** — no fragments, no run-ons.
- ☐ I **whisper-read** it, and it all makes sense (no contradictions).
- ☐ Every source is **cited** and on the **Works Cited** page — no leftover placeholders.
- ☐ I reread the **directions** and did **all** of them.

**Done and checked beats long and rushed. Reread the directions, tick the last box, then upload it on time.**
