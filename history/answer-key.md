## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Grow it and check it") in the [study guide](guide.html).

| # | Answer | Skill | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **What · When · Where · How it mattered** | Details | A full answer is the title plus those four details. The last one is the one most kids skip. |
| 2 | e.g. *"The library opened downtown in 2022. It gave students a free place to study after school. More kids started coming in to read."* | Details | Adds **where** (downtown) and **how it mattered** (a free study spot). Any answer with those two counts. |
| 3 | e.g. *"The ship sailed all the way around the world."* | Own words | Said plainly, in his own words — not the source's wording. |
| 4 | **"She became the first woman to fly across the ocean."** | Check it | Fragment — no WHO. Add the who + did what. |
| 5 | **"The park opened in 1890. It was the first of its kind."** | Check it | Run-on — split the two complete ideas with a period (or join with ", and"). |
| 6 | **Nothing is wrong — it's complete** | Check it | What (Moon landing), when (1969), where (U.S. mission/the Moon), and why it mattered are all there, in 3 complete sentences. The trick: don't "fix" work that's already done. |
| 7 | **It's not finished — replace the placeholder with the real Works Cited list before uploading** | Check it | A leftover placeholder is a sign the work wasn't reread. |
| 8 | **"The bridge cut the trip to the hospital from an hour to 10 minutes"** | Details | Impact = what changed for people. "The bridge was long" is a description, not an effect. |

**Coaching tip (point to the step, not the mistake):** instead of "you didn't finish #2,"
say "run it through the **4 W's** — did you hit *where* and *how it mattered*?" and let him
find the gap. When he writes one thin sentence, don't rewrite it for him — ask "what's the
*where*? what *changed* because of it?" and let him grow it. Praise the checking ("you
caught that run-on yourself") as much as the facts. Biggest win for him: **reread the
directions out loud before he uploads** — that single habit is what the teacher flagged.
