## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Spot it, fix it") in the [study guide](guide.html).

| # | Fix | Type | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | or → **nor** | Conjunction | "Neither" pairs with "nor." |
| 2 | in → **at** | Preposition | Set phrase: good **at** something. |
| 3 | Ouch → **Hooray** | Interjection | Winning is good news; match a happy word. |
| 4 | but → **so** | Conjunction | Not studying caused failing — show the result with "so." |
| 5 | at → **on** | Preposition | Use "on" with days of the week: on Friday. |
| 6 | **No mistake!** | Preposition | "Under" is the right word for where the cat is. |
| 7 | unless → **until** | Conjunction | Until = up to that time; unless = except if. |
| 8 | cross out **of** | Preposition | "Off" already does the job: jumped off the counter. |
| 9 | between → **among** | Preposition | Six players is more than two, so use among. |
| 10 | **Hey!** Watch out… (or **Hey,** watch out…) | Interjection | An interjection needs a ! or a comma after it. |

**Coaching tip:** When he misses one, don't give the answer. Ask the cheat-card question —
"What does the join mean?" or "Is that the right set phrase?" — and let him find it.
