## The 3-pass method (one pass per type)

1. **Count first.** Look at the circles and write the totals in the margin.
2. **Pass 1 — Conjunctions:** read looking only at joining words (*and, but, or, so, nor*).
3. **Pass 2 — Prepositions:** read again, looking only at little position words (*in, on, at, to, from*).
4. **Pass 3 — Interjections:** read once more for the feeling words (*Wow, Ouch, Hooray*).
5. **Read it out loud.** If a sentence sounds off, a mistake is still hiding.

**Not done until every circle is crossed off.**

## Conjunctions: joining words

A conjunction connects words or ideas. Pick the one that matches the **meaning**.

| Word | Job | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **and** | adds | I have a dog **and** a cat. |
| **but** | contrasts (surprise) | I'm tired, **but** I'll help. |
| **or** | a choice | Tea **or** coffee? |
| **so** | a result | It rained, **so** we stayed in. |

### Trap 1: the wrong meaning

| Wrong | Right | Clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I wanted to play, **and** it rained. | I wanted to play, **but** it rained. | the two ideas disagree → **but** |
| He didn't study, **but** he failed. | He didn't study, **so** he failed. | cause → result → **so** |

### Trap 2: correlative pairs must match

Some conjunctions come in **pairs**. Keep the partners together:

- **neither … nor** (She is neither tall **nor** short.)
- **either … or** (Either you clean **or** you lose it.)
- **both … and** · **not only … but also**

> ~~neither tall **or** short~~ → neither tall **nor** short.

More pairs to keep together: **whether … or** (I can't decide **whether** to stay **or** go.)

### Trap 3: starter words (although, because, until, unless, when, after)

These joining words often **start** a sentence. Check the meaning, then put a **comma
where the first part ends**.

| Word | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **although** | even though (a surprise) | **Although** it was cold, we swam. |
| **because** | the reason | **Because** the bus was late, we ran. |
| **until** | up to that time | We waited **until** the rain stopped. |
| **unless** | except if | We'll go **unless** it storms. |
| **when / after** | the time | **After** we ate, we left. |

| Wrong | Right | Clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We stayed inside **unless** the storm passed. | We stayed inside **until** the storm passed. | up to that time → **until** |
| **Although** I was hungry, I ate a snack. | **Because** I was hungry, I ate a snack. | it's the reason, not a surprise → **because** |

## Prepositions: little position words

Prepositions show **where, when, or direction**: *in, on, at, to, from, under, over*.
Many belong to **set phrases** you just have to know:

| Say this | Not this |
| --- | --- |
| good **at** math | ~~good in math~~ |
| arrive **at** school | ~~arrive to school~~ |
| interested **in** science | ~~interested on science~~ |
| different **from** | ~~different than~~ |
| **on** Friday · **in** June · **at** 3:00 | ~~at Friday~~ |

### Extra prepositions: cross them out

Sometimes the mistake is a word that **shouldn't be there at all**. Read it without the
little word. If it still makes sense, cross it out.

| Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- |
| jumped off **of** the bed | jumped off the bed |
| Where is my hat **at**? | Where is my hat? |
| sat beside **of** the lake | sat beside the lake |
| I met **up with** my friends. | I met my friends. |

### between vs. among

- **between** = **2** things (between the two trees)
- **among** = **3 or more** (among all the kids)

## Interjections: feeling words

An interjection shows sudden feeling and usually has a comma or an exclamation point:
**Wow! Ouch! Hooray! Ugh! Oh no!** The feeling should **match the moment**.

| Wrong | Right | Clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hooray,** I lost my homework! | **Oh no,** I lost my homework! | bad news → an "uh-oh" word |
| **Ouch,** we won the game! | **Hooray,** we won the game! | good news → a happy word |

### Give it a ! or a ,

An interjection never just runs into the sentence.

- Strong feeling → **!** and a new sentence: **Ouch!** That hurt.
- Mild feeling → **,** and keep going: **Hmm,** I'm not sure.

> ~~Wow the stars were bright.~~ → **Wow!** The stars were bright. *(or **Wow,** the stars…)*

## Cheat card (cut out and keep by the homework)

|  | Ask yourself | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Conjunction** | What does the join MEAN? | and/but/or/so · pairs: neither…nor, either…or |
| **Preposition** | Is it the right set phrase? | good at · arrive at · interested in · different from · on + day |
| **Interjection** | Does the feeling match? | Wow!/Ouch!/Hooray!/Oh no! · comma or ! |

**Also watch for:** extra words to cross out (off ~~of~~, where … ~~at~~, met ~~up with~~) ·
between = 2, among = 3+ · starter words (although, until, unless) need the right meaning and a comma.
