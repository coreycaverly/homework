**Unit 5.** Two amounts are **proportional** when one is always the **same number times**
the other. That number is the **constant of proportionality** — it's just a unit rate.

## The big idea: y = k · x

**k** = the constant of proportionality = **y ÷ x** (the amount for ONE x).

> 3 apples cost $6, 5 apples cost $10, 8 apples cost $16. Each time, cost = **2 × apples**,
> so k = **2** ($2 per apple), and y = 2x.

## Spot it three ways

| In a… | It's proportional when… |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | you multiply x by the **same number** every time to get y (y ÷ x is constant) |
| **Equation** | it looks like **y = kx** (no + or − number added on) |
| **Graph** | it's a **straight line through the origin (0, 0)** |

### Trap: it must pass through (0, 0)

y = 2x is proportional. y = 2x **+ 3** is **not** — the +3 means 0 x's don't give 0 y's,
and the graph misses the origin.

## Using k

- **Find k:** divide any y by its x. (6 ÷ 3 = 2.)
- **Find a missing y:** y = k · x. (k = 2, x = 10 → y = 20.)
- **Find a missing x:** x = y ÷ k. (k = 2, y = 24 → x = 12.)

## Scale (a kind of proportion)

A **scale factor** multiplies every length. Scale factor 3 → every side is 3× longer.
Scale copies keep the same shape; only the size changes. "1 cm = 5 km" is a scale too.

## Write a strong answer (ACE)

Right answers still lose points when they're a bare number or a fragment. Show your
thinking the way every teacher wants — with **ACE**:

- **A — Answer** by restating the question in a complete sentence. *(Don't START with
  "Because." Restate first, then give your "because.")*
- **C — Calculate / show it** with **labels and units** — "$30," "20 square units,"
  "$1.20 per box" — not just "30."
- **E — Explain** the *why* with the rule or formula you used.

| Weak | Strong (ACE) |
| --- | --- |
| *30.* | *The area is 30 square units, because ½ × 10 × 6 = 30.* |
| *Yes.* | *Yes, 6:8 equals 3:4, because dividing both by 2 gives 3 and 4.* |
| *$10.* | *The sale price is $30, because 25% of $40 is $10 off, and $40 − $10 = $30.* |

> **Then check it:** reread the question and make sure you answered **what it actually
> asked** — not just the first number you found.

## Practice

1. A table: 2 → 10, 3 → 15, 5 → 25. What is k?   2. With that k, what is y when x = 8?
3. Is y = 4x + 1 proportional? Why?   4. A map scale is 1 cm = 20 km. How far is 4 cm?

## STOP & CHECK

- ☐ I found k by **dividing y by x**.
- ☐ For "is it proportional?" I checked the ratio is the **same** AND it hits **(0,0)**.
- ☐ I labeled my answer.
