**Unit 6.** Percent means **"per 100."** This unit is all the real-money stuff: discounts,
tax, tips, and markups — finding a percent of a number, then adding or taking it away.

## Percent of a number

Turn the percent into a decimal (**÷ 100**, move the point 2 left), then **multiply**.

| Percent | Decimal | 20% of 50 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20% | 0.20 | 0.20 × 50 = **10** |
| 7% | 0.07 | 0.07 × 50 = **3.5** |
| 150% | 1.50 | 1.50 × 50 = **75** |

## Increase and decrease (two steps)

1. Find the change: **percent × original**.
2. **Add** it (increase: tax, tip, markup) or **subtract** it (decrease: discount, sale).

> **$40 shirt, 25% off:** change = 0.25 × 40 = $10, so new price = 40 − 10 = **$30**.
> **$40 meal, 18% tip:** change = 0.18 × 40 = $7.20, so total = 40 + 7.20 = **$47.20**.

**Fast way (one step):**
- Increase by 15% → × **1.15**.  Decrease by 15% → × **0.85**.
- ($40 shirt, 25% off → 40 × 0.75 = $30.)

## Finding the percent change

**percent change = change ÷ original × 100.**

> Price goes $20 → $25. Change = $5. 5 ÷ 20 = 0.25 = **25% increase**.

## Write a strong answer (ACE)

Right answers still lose points when they're a bare number or a fragment. Show your
thinking the way every teacher wants — with **ACE**:

- **A — Answer** by restating the question in a complete sentence. *(Don't START with
  "Because." Restate first, then give your "because.")*
- **C — Calculate / show it** with **labels and units** — "$30," "20 square units,"
  "$1.20 per box" — not just "30."
- **E — Explain** the *why* with the rule or formula you used.

| Weak | Strong (ACE) |
| --- | --- |
| *30.* | *The area is 30 square units, because ½ × 10 × 6 = 30.* |
| *Yes.* | *Yes, 6:8 equals 3:4, because dividing both by 2 gives 3 and 4.* |
| *$10.* | *The sale price is $30, because 25% of $40 is $10 off, and $40 − $10 = $30.* |

> **Then check it:** reread the question and make sure you answered **what it actually
> asked** — not just the first number you found.

## Cheat card

|  | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Percent → decimal** | move the point 2 places left (20% = 0.20) |
| **Percent of a number** | decimal × number |
| **Discount / sale** | subtract the change (or × (1 − %)) |
| **Tax / tip / markup** | add the change (or × (1 + %)) |
| **What percent change?** | change ÷ **original** × 100 |

## Practice

1. 30% of 90?   2. A $60 jacket is 20% off — final price?   3. A $25 bill with 8% tax — total?
4. A plant grows from 40 cm to 50 cm — what percent increase?

## STOP & CHECK

- ☐ I turned the percent into a **decimal** before multiplying.
- ☐ For change, I divided by the **original** amount.
- ☐ I **added** for tax/tip, **subtracted** for a discount.
