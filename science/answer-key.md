## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Answer it, explain it") in the [study guide](./).

| # | Answer | Topic | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **direction** | Motion | Velocity = speed AND direction. |
| 2 | the **empty** cart | Newton's Laws | Same force, less mass = more acceleration (a = F ÷ m). |
| 3 | **5 m/s²** | Calculations | a = F ÷ m = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 m/s². |
| 4 | **3rd Law** (action–reaction) | Newton's Laws | Push water back → water pushes you forward. |
| 5 | **30 N right** | Calculations | Opposite forces subtract: 45 − 15 = 30 N, toward the bigger one. |
| 6 | They act on **two different objects** | Newton's Laws | Action and reaction can't cancel because they're on different objects. |

**Coaching tip:** When he misses one, don't give the answer. Ask the cheat-card
question ("Same push — which has less mass?" or "What's the formula?") and have him
finish the sentence with *"because…"*.
