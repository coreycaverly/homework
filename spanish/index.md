## Números 0–30

Count up and say them aloud.

| 0–10 | 11–15 (irregular!) | 16–19 | 20s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez | **once, doce, trece, catorce, quince** | dieciséis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve | veinte, veintiuno, veintidós… |

### Trap: 11–15 are their OWN words — not "dieci-"

| Wrong | Right | Clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~diecitrés~~ (13) | **trece** | 11–15 don't use dieci- |
| ~~diecicinco~~ (15) | **quince** | the dieci- pattern starts at **16** |

> **Say it back:** "once, doce, trece, catorce, quince… THEN dieciséis." The jump to
> dieci- happens at 16.

## Días y meses

- **Días:** lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves, viernes, sábado, domingo.
- **Meses:** enero, febrero, marzo, abril, mayo, junio, julio, agosto, septiembre,
  octubre, noviembre, diciembre.

*(Days and months are lowercase in Spanish.)*

## El clima (weather)

| Symbol | Spanish | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ☀️ | **Hace sol** | It's sunny |
| 🌧️ | **Está lloviendo** | It's raining |
| ❄️ | **Nieva** / Está nevando | It's snowing |
| ☁️ | **Está nublado** | It's cloudy |

## En la clase (classroom words)

**Hay un(a)…** = There is a… · **No hay…** = There isn't…

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| el lápiz | pencil |
| el bolígrafo | pen |
| las tijeras | scissors |
| el pegamento | glue |
| la goma / el borrador | eraser |
| el sacapuntas | pencil sharpener |
| el papel | paper |

## Sobre mí (about me)

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| **Me gusta** el fútbol. | I like soccer. |
| **No me gusta** dormir. | I don't like sleeping. |
| **Tengo once años.** | I am 11 years old. *(use tener = to have)* |
| **Mi cumpleaños es** el 28 de abril. | My birthday is April 28. |
| **Soy de** los Estados Unidos. | I'm from the United States. |
