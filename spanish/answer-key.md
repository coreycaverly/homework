## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Say it, then write it") in the [study guide](guide.html).

| # | Answer | Topic | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **trece** | Números | 13 is irregular — its own word, not "dieci-". |
| 2 | **quince** | Números | 15 is irregular; dieci- only starts at 16. |
| 3 | **Wednesday** | Calendario | lunes, martes, miércoles… |
| 4 | **Hace sol** | Calendario | Sunny = Hace sol. |
| 5 | **scissors** | Vocabulario | tijeras = scissors. |
| 6 | **Tengo once años** | Vocabulario | Age uses tener: "I have 11 years." |

**Coaching tip:** Have him count *once, doce, trece, catorce, quince* out loud, then
pause before *dieciséis*. That "pause at 16" is where the dieci- pattern begins.
