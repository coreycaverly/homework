## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Pick the stronger answer") in the [study guide](guide.html).

| # | Answer | Skill | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Fragment** | Sentences | No who + did what. Fix: "Jake got in trouble for running down the hall after lunch." |
| 2 | **too** | Homophones | too = very / also. |
| 3 | **They're / their** | Homophones | They're = they are; their = they own it. |
| 4 | **My team lost the game, but we still had fun.** | Sentences | Split with a period, or join two complete ideas with ", but". |
| 5 | **Restate the question in a complete sentence** | Explain It | Start with "The answer is…" — never start with "Because." |
| 6 | **The math and the why** | Explain It | ACE needs Calculate + Explain: "$1.50 vs $1.40 per notebook, so B costs less." |

**Coaching tip (point to the checklist, not the mistake):** instead of "you forgot a
capital," say "check your **C** and **P** again," and let him find it. Praise the
slowing down ("I saw you whisper-read that") as much as the right answer. If he rushed,
don't redo the whole page — have him run **one** sentence through *who + did what →
whisper-read → check* out loud, then he earns the star.
