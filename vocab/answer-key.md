## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Use it") in the [study guide](guide.html).

| # | Answer | Skill | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Going on without stopping. | Meaning | Any own-words version with "without a break/stop" works. |
| 2 | To think up (a plan). | Which meaning? | Not "come out of an egg": you hatch a plan in your head. |
| 3 | Very tiny. | Which meaning? | Said *my-NOOT* here, not the 60-second *minute*. |
| 4 | **motionless** | Word family | Standing still = without motion. |
| 5 | e.g. *The sun began to emerge from behind the clouds after the storm.* | Use it | Any complete sentence where the clues show "come into view." |
| 6 | **domesticated** | Word family | It describes the animal, so use the describing form. |

**Coaching tip:** Ask him to say a meaning in his own words before he writes. If a
sentence is thin ("The bear emerged."), ask "From where? When?" and let him add the clue.
