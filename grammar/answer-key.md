## Answer key (for parents)

For the practice section ("Spot it, fix it") in the [study guide](guide.html).

| # | Fix | Type | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | quick → **quickly** | ADV | how did it run? |
| 2 | faster → **fastest** | ADJ | whole team = 3 or more |
| 3 | a → **an** elephant | ART | vowel sound |
| 4 | danger → **dangerous** | ADJ | noun used as a describer |
| 5 | enoughly → **enough** | ADV | fake -ly |
| 6 | Tomorrow → **Yesterday** | ADV | "went" already happened |
| 7 | good → **well** | ADV | how did she do? |
| 8 | best → **better** | ADJ | only two pies |
| 9 | a → **the** brightest | ART | there's only one brightest |
| 10 | terribly → **terrible** | ADJ | after "smelled" (linking verb) |
| 11 | a → **an** extremely | ART | vowel sound |
| 12 | worse → **worst** | ADJ | "ever" = -est |
| 13 | **No error!** | ART | "hour" starts with a vowel sound, so "an" is right |

**Coaching tip:** When he misses one, don't give the answer. Ask the question from the cheat card ("How did she do it?") and let him find it.
